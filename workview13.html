DiagramAR
UI
2020

As many math and computer science students have experienced, trying to understand complex 3- dimensional diagrams displayed on a 2-dimensional textbook page can be quite confusing. Especially for those of us that are more comfortable learning visually rather than textually, this can significantly hinder our ability to learn the course material. DiagramAR attempts to solve this problem by efficiently converting 2D figures from the lecture notes into immersive AR diagrams in real time.

This app was originally developed at Boston University to supplement an introductory linear algebra course. It currently supports visualizations for fundamental linear algebra concepts, such as vectors/lines, curves, planes, and curved surfaces.
